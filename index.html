四六级语法考点
高频语法专项突破
1. 虚拟语气 CET-6

表示假设、愿望、建议等非真实情况。四六级翻译和写作高频考点。

If I were you, I would accept the offer.

It is essential that he (should) attend the meeting.

2. 倒装句 CET-4

部分倒装或完全倒装，常见于四六级翻译和写作。

Not only did he finish the work, but also he helped others.

Only in this way can we solve the problem.

3. 强调句 CET-4

It is/was...that/who... 强调句型。

It was in Beijing that I first met her.

It is hard work that leads to success.

4. 非谓语动词 CET-6

不定式、动名词、分词的用法区别。

Having finished the work, he went home.

I look forward to hearing from you.

5. 主谓一致 CET-4

就近原则、意义一致等规则。

Neither he nor I am going to the party.

The number of students is increasing.

四六级翻译技巧
常见翻译策略与方法
1. 词性转换

中文多用动词，英文多用名词和介词。如"他善于交际" → "He is good at communication."

2. 增词与减词

英文需要添加冠词、介词等。如"读书" → "read books"（添加复数）。

3. 语态转换

中文多用主动，英文多用被动。如"人们认为…" → "It is believed that..."

四六级常用句型与结构
开头段模板

Recently, the issue of ... has aroused widespread concern. As far as I am concerned, ...

正文段模板

There are several reasons accounting for this phenomenon. First and foremost, ... Moreover, ... Last but not least, ...

On the one hand, ... On the other hand, ... Therefore, it is advisable to ...

结尾段模板

In conclusion, ... Only by doing so can we ...

To sum up, ... It is high time that we took effective measures to ...

高级替换词

important → significant / crucial / essential

more and more → an increasing number of

I think → from my perspective / as far as I am concerned

范文参考
参考范文（点击换题后更新）
In recent years, the concept of lifelong learning has gained increasing attention in our society. As far as I am concerned, lifelong learning is of great importance for the following reasons. First and foremost, in today's rapidly changing world, knowledge becomes outdated very quickly. What we learned in school may no longer be sufficient to meet the demands of our careers. Therefore, we must keep learning to stay competitive. Moreover, lifelong learning contributes to personal growth and self-fulfillment. By acquiring new skills and knowledge, we can broaden our horizons and enrich our lives. In conclusion, lifelong learning is essential for both professional development and personal satisfaction. Only by embracing the concept of lifelong learning can we adapt to the ever-changing society and lead a meaningful life.
